# Audiences — archetypes as jobs-to-be-done

## A — The commissioning professional

Filmmaker, author, journalist, podcast researcher. Needs research that has to be right in public;
judges rigour signals and handover artifacts (research memo, annotated source ledger,
counterargument file, terminology sheet).

- **Arrives from:** peer referral, "research service for documentary/book/podcast" search, a brief
  excerpt that impressed a colleague, LinkedIn/newsletter.
- **Job to be done:** "Buy certainty I can defend on camera or in print, without becoming an
  expert myself — and see exactly what I'll receive before I commit ₹25,000."
- **Success behaviour (observable):** reaches Studio via home or nav, verifies capacity signal
  ("N of 3 slots"), inspects a handover-artifact list or brief anatomy, then starts the commission
  form or notes pricing without bouncing. Failure mode: leaves after the hero because nothing on
  screen proves work product exists.
- **What repels them:** vague portfolios, testimonial theatre, hidden pricing, urgency tricks,
  anything smelling of AI-generated content farm.

## B — The serious reader

Intellectually ambitious, allergic to self-help tone; Brief 001 buyer; future Codex cohort member.

- **Arrives from:** shared excerpt, the Primer sequence, a dangerous-question mention ("Can
  Vimānas Fly?"), organic search on a specific itch (desire, purity, identity persistence).
- **Job to be done:** "Give me a structure that explains why getting what I wanted didn't settle
  anything — argued from evidence, without preaching, that I can argue with."
- **Success behaviour (observable):** opens Brief 001, reads the sample, checks the unresolved
  list, buys (once checkout exists) or subscribes to the Primer meanwhile; returns for the letter.
  Failure mode: skims the contents list, finds no readable proof of depth, leaves.
- **What repels them:** self-help register, moralising, mystery-tease paywalls hiding the
  argument, devotional aesthetics, engagement-bait mechanics.

## C — The cold skeptic

Arrives from a share, decides in seconds. The site passes only if a curious atheist could nod at
every **surface-layer** sentence (layer discipline, constitution L6).

- **Arrives from:** a shared quote/screenshot, a group chat link, social mention of a brief topic.
- **Job to be done:** "Decide in ~5 seconds whether this is credible work or dressed-up belief —
  and have my scepticism respected either way."
- **Success behaviour (observable):** in the 5-second test correctly answers "research outfit /
  shows its sources / sells investigated essays"; clicks through to method/docs/brief rather than
  bouncing; on reaching deeper layers, finds the framework *labelled as framework* rather than
  smuggled — which paradoxically raises trust. Failure mode: pattern-matches the cream-serif look
  to generic AI content sites and dismisses before reading a word.
- **What repels them:** unverifiable claims, assumed agreement, urgency/scarcity theatre, visual
  clichés that read as low-effort content marketing, any whiff of the devotional register at the
  surface.

## D — The ambitious builder *(added 2026-08-25 from ARK_Vision_Document.md §1–2)*

Student, programmer, athlete, founder, designer — the vision's primary emotional audience. Comes
for capability, not research services; may become a Brief buyer later and a Codex cohort member
eventually.

- **Arrives from:** social shares of dangerous questions, "how to think" content, peer
  recommendation, search on self-development adjacent to a discipline they already love.
- **Job to be done:** "Become extraordinarily capable in my own dimension without becoming
  spiritually empty or intellectually narrow — show me what that path looks like concretely."
- **Success behaviour (observable):** experiences trajectory-expansion ("I could become more than
  I planned") anchored to real artefacts (briefs, method, counter) rather than vibes; subscribes
  to the Primer or joins the Codex waitlist; returns for the weekly letter. Failure mode:
  pattern-matches to motivation content, distrusts it, leaves — or worse, consumes and forgets.
- **What repels them:** hustle-culture vocabulary and transformation theatre (anti-list #13),
  religious branding that demands identity conversion up front, SaaS gamification, being flattered
  instead of equipped. Dignity-first tone is mandatory for this archetype: equip, never inflame.
